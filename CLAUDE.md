# HABIBI - Marrakech Restaurant - Coming Soon Website

## Project
Scroll-driven animated website for HABIBI, a luxury show-dinner restaurant
in Marrakech. Currently under construction. Website purpose: client proposal
and coming soon page.

## Assets
- `assets/habibi-video.mp4` — AI-rendered walkthrough video.
  Sequence: exterior door with curtains closed → curtains open → 
  walk inside → close-up on plate with food
- `assets/logo.webp` — HABIBI logo

## Skill
.claude/skills/animated-website/ — use this skill for the full build

## Design
- Font: Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif — NO Google Fonts, no external loading
- Background: #0a0808
- Accent: #8B1A1A (red velvet)
- Secondary accent: #5C0F0F (deeper red for glows/hover states)
- Mood: luxury, cinematic, dark, provocative. NOT warm gold, NOT generic hospitality clichés

## Copy Direction
Provocative coming soon. No specific dates, no menu info, no specific details.
Tone: confident, desire-building, minimal. Think luxury fashion brand, not restaurant chain.
Name displayed: HABIBI
Location: Marrakech
Examples of the right tone: "We could open tomorrow. We won't." / "Not yet. But you'll feel it."

## Scroll Narrative (align dwell points to these moments)
1. Exterior — curtains closed, mystery
2. Curtains opening — the reveal
3. Interior — the atmosphere
4. Plate close-up — the promise
